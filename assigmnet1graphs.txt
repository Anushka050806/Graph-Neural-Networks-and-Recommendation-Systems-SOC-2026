When the total energy E is increased much further above the potential V, the quantity:

k = sqrt(2(E - V))becomes larger.

This increases the frequency of oscillation of the wavefunction. Physically, a higher-energy particle has a shorter wavelength and therefore oscillates more rapidly in space.

CASE B: Physically, this corresponds to a thick potential barrier. The electron has only a very small probability of penetrating deeply into the barrier.

Accuracy: represents total number of correct predictions divided by total number of cases. Because roughly 86.4% of dataset is on-time, a useless model that blindly guesses "On-Time" for every single submission will still achieve 86.4% accuracy.

Precision: Precision answers: our precision is 0, the model never flagged a late submission correctly .

Recall: Recall answers:  Practical Meaning: A recall of 0.0000 means the system is completely blind to late submissions. If an operations team relied on this, 100% of late submissions would slip through the cracks unnoticed.

F1-Score : The harmonic mean of Precision and Recall. It acts as a balanced metric for minority classes. A score of 0 indicates the model has zero functional predictive power for late submissions.

AUC-ROC: This measures the model's ability to distinguish between the two classes across various decision thresholds. A score of 0.50 represents purely random guessing (like flipping a coin).
